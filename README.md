# AI Wealth Insider — AI Investor Brain

This page gives your AI assistant its instructions for **Module 1, Lesson 3**: playing a skeptical but fair investor on
your own business workbook, then coaching you on your ninety-day plan.

**How to use it:** follow the prompt card in your lesson materials. Your AI reads `brain.md` from this link:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aiwealthinsider/ai-investor-brain/main/brain.md
If your AI can't open links, copy the text of `brain.md` and paste it into the chat instead.

Never paste client or patient data into an AI. An AI's critique is not proof: only real customers can answer the
questions it raises.
